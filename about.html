The Shekhawat Packers Story
Headquartered in the bustling commercial heart of Gandhi Nagar, East Delhi—Asia's largest readymade garment and textile trading hub—Shekhawat Packers has grown from a local corrugated box supplier into one of Delhi NCR's most respected wholesale packaging material distribution houses.

Founded and led by Himmat Singh, our mission has remained steadfast: provide manufacturers, exporters, e-commerce brands, and local retailers with durable, factory-grade packaging materials at transparent wholesale prices, backed by immediate local availability.
Direct Factory Pricing: Cut out retail middlemen and traders to save 15-25% on ongoing packing costs.
Tested Material Strength: Guaranteed Burst Factor (BF), high GSM kraft paper, and high-tack acrylic adhesives.
Ready Buffer Inventory: Tens of thousands of boxes and tape rolls maintained in stock for same-day dispatch.
Full GST Compliance: Transparent B2B invoicing enabling your company to claim 100% input tax credit (ITC).
